Stephanie Yu 15-112 Term Project: Fruit Ninja

Short description:
The name of my term project is Fruit Ninja. It is a game where the player will have to move or 
drag the mouse as a virtual blade to slice different types of fruit that appear on the screen. 
The game features two modes (Classic and Zen) and the goal is to score as many points as possible 
by slicing as many fruit as you can without missing any or hitting any bombs. In Classic mode, 
you have 3 lives and every time the player misses a fruit, the player will lose one life. The game 
ends when you run out of lives or you hit a bomb. In Zen mode, you have unlimited lives and there are 
no bombs, but you have 60 seconds to slice up as many fruits as you can. You can also change the 
color of your blade and the background image of the game mode in the Settings menu. 
After the game ends, you can view your score and try to beat your high score in the next game!
